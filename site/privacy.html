privacy

What we do with your data

Short version: nothing.

pondlog.co uses your browser’s geolocation only when you tap use my location in the hero. We do not store it. We do not send it anywhere except to api.cropgraph.com to compute your planting zone, and only when you opt in.

We set no cookies. We collect no analytics. We do not track return visits.

The garden line in the hero calls api.cropgraph.com with the latitude and longitude you provided. CropGraph keeps approximate request counts for rate limiting and does not log the underlying coordinates.

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel keeps its own infrastructure logs as documented at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy. We do not access those logs.

The site is fully open source. Read the source at github.com/andrewschristison/pondlog/tree/main/site.
